# Tilemap Demo

## Concept
<!-- id: concept -->
Demonstration of Forge's 2D chunked tilemap rendering and modification APIs.

## Pillars
<!-- id: pillars -->
- High-performance chunked tile streaming
- Dynamic runtime tile painting

## Core Loop
<!-- id: core-loop -->
Paint, modify, and query tile layers in real-time.

## Controls
<!-- id: controls -->
Mouse clicks to place or remove tiles.

## Art Direction
<!-- id: art-direction -->
Grid-aligned tileset sprites.

## Audio Direction
<!-- id: audio-direction -->
Tile placement audio feedback.

## Scope Limits
<!-- id: scope-limits -->
Tilemap showcase environment.
